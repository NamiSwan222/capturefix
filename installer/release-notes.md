## CaptureFix 1.4.0

**AI upscaling with NVIDIA RTX.**

- **AI upscaling.** On an NVIDIA RTX graphics card, CaptureFix can now use NVIDIA's RTX Video Super
  Resolution: the AI redraws the 1080p picture at your screen size. Edges, text and fine textures
  come out cleaner than with a normal scaler, and the colours stay the same.
- Turn it on with **Picture > AI upscaling (NVIDIA RTX)** on the control bar, or in Settings >
  Picture. **Ultra** mode turns it on by itself.
- It needs **Super Resolution** switched on in the NVIDIA app (System > Video > RTX Video
  Enhancement). If the switch is off, CaptureFix tells you in the Picture menu and keeps using its
  own sharp scaler.
- Works with SDR pictures shown larger than the console sends them. HDR pictures keep the sharp scaler.
