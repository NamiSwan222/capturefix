## CaptureFix 1.3.0

**Controls on screen, and an Ultra picture mode.**

- **Control bar.** The everyday controls now sit in a bar under the picture: console, picture mode,
  smooth motion, signal, volume, screenshot, full screen and settings. No more hunting through the
  right-click menu (it is still there, under the "..." button).
- **Minimize it.** The arrow at the right end of the bar, or Tab, hides it. A small "Controls" tab
  comes back while you move the mouse, so nothing shows in a shared Discord window. In full screen
  the bar hides by itself when the mouse rests.
- **Picture modes: Original, Balanced, Ultra.** Ultra gives the sharpest picture and the smoothest
  motion: sharp upscaling, adaptive sharpening (no halos), smooth gradients (no banding in skies
  and fog), extra clarity and frame generation. None of it changes the colours.
- Each enhancer has its own slider in Settings > Picture > Picture mode.
- Menus are dark now, like the rest of the app.
